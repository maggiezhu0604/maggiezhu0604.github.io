# Week 2: Quantitative Genetics

## Focus

This week covers the genetic basis of complex traits, including variance components, heritability, selection response, and breeding values.

## Learning Goals

- Explain why many agronomic traits are quantitative.
- Define additive genetic variance, environmental variance, and phenotypic variance.
- Interpret broad-sense and narrow-sense heritability.
- Connect selection response to breeding program design.

## Suggested Activities

- Work through simple heritability calculations.
- Compare qualitative and quantitative traits.
- Summarize how breeding values support selection.

## Portfolio Output

Create `heritability-and-selection.md` with:

- Key definitions.
- One worked example.
- A short discussion of why heritability can change across environments.
