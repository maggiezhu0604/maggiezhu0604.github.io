# Week 4: Phenotypes And Environments

## Focus

This week studies field trial phenotypes, environmental covariates, experimental design, and data quality challenges in multi-environment trials.

## Learning Goals

- Describe common agronomic traits such as yield, flowering time, and plant height.
- Explain why field trial design matters.
- Identify environmental variables relevant to crop performance.
- Recognize missing data, outliers, and metadata issues.

## Suggested Activities

- Draft a trait data dictionary.
- List environmental covariates that might influence maize performance.
- Review examples of multi-environment trial layouts.

## Portfolio Output

Create `trait-environment-data-dictionary.md` with:

- Trait names and biological meaning.
- Units and expected value ranges.
- Environment metadata needed for genotype-by-environment analysis.
