# Week 7: Genotype-By-Environment Interaction And G2F

## Focus

This week prepares the final project by studying genotype-by-environment interaction and the Genomes to Fields initiative.

## Learning Goals

- Define genotype-by-environment interaction.
- Explain why GxE matters for cultivar recommendation and selection.
- Understand the broad structure of G2F genotype, phenotype, and environment data.
- Draft a final project question using G2F.

## Suggested Activities

- Review the available G2F data documentation.
- Identify candidate traits, environments, and years for analysis.
- Draft a project proposal with research question, data, methods, and expected outputs.

## Portfolio Output

Create `g2f-project-proposal.md` with:

- Research question.
- Dataset plan.
- Candidate prediction methods.
- Expected figures and tables.
- Risks, assumptions, and fallback options.
