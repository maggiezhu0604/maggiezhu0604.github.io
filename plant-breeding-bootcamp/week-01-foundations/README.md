# Week 1: Foundations Of Plant Breeding

## Focus

This week introduces the purpose of plant breeding, the structure of breeding programs, and the relationship between phenotype, genotype, environment, and selection.

## Learning Goals

- Explain what plant breeders try to improve and why.
- Describe a basic breeding pipeline from population development to variety release.
- Distinguish phenotype, genotype, environment, and management.
- Identify where computational analysis supports breeding decisions.

## Suggested Activities

- Read an introductory chapter on plant breeding goals and breeding methods.
- Create a concept map of the breeding pipeline.
- Write a short summary of one crop improvement example.

## Portfolio Output

Create `breeding-pipeline-summary.md` with:

- A plain-language overview of plant breeding.
- A diagram or bullet list of the breeding cycle.
- Three examples of data generated in modern breeding programs.
