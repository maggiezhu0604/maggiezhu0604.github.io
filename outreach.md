My outreach work centers on science communication, food safety education, sustainability leadership, and community-engaged research.

**UC Global Food Initiative Fellowship**
As a UC Global Food Initiative Research Fellow, I contributed to a community-engaged project studying heavy metal contamination in edible plants from community garden and food desert contexts in Southern California and northern Mexico.

**Community Garden Soil Testing Project**
The project analyzed plant and soil samples, including more than 200 edible plant samples, and translated results into practical information for growers and community partners.

**Food Safety Education**
I helped organize soil and plant tissue testing outreach programs and communicated results to community gardeners so residents could better understand contamination risks and possible mitigation approaches.

**Sustainability Resource Center Leadership**
As Student Director of Operation and Resources at the UCSD Sustainability Resource Center, I coordinated staff operations, hiring, ordering, and university compliance; trained new staff; and mentored interns on independent projects.

**Science Communication and Community Engagement**
I helped initiate programs including a Reusable Dishware Program, Office Library, and Food Pantry, and participated in workshops that connected plant science data with public-facing food safety education.

**Food Safety and Plant Data Translation**
This work strengthened my interest in plant data that leads to usable decisions, especially when research affects food security, sustainability, and community health.
