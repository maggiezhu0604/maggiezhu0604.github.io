Objective

Conclusion. This synthesis evaluates whether DNA foundation-model-derived variant scores can be converted into genotype-level functional priors that explain G2F maize yield variation beyond frozen genotype/environment baselines and matched negative controls.

Motivation

Evidence. The project tested the bridge from DNA sequence variation to molecular or regulatory functional prior, genotype-level burden or kernel, and agronomic GxE prediction.

Inference. The central scientific value is a controlled test of whether sequence-derived priors survive leakage-controlled, out-of-environment and matched-control evaluation.

Methods

Evidence. PlantCAD2 scores were consumed through marker weights, SNP kernels, GxE kernel diagnostics, fixed 5Mb burden features and frozen M61 residual-rescue diagnostics.

Evidence. AgroNT scores used ref-alt embedding delta, rank-normalized mean-1 positive weights, fixed 5Mb block burdens, genomewide burden covariates and matched controls.

Boundary. M20 does not change transforms, reopen tuning, make a leaderboard claim or validate molecular function.

Conclusion. Global claim label: multi_model_diagnostic_negative. No tested foundation-model functional prior passed its frozen diagnostic claim gate.

Discussion

Inference. The shared failure mode is consistent with a sparse-marker functional-prior bottleneck: foundation-model scores can be generated and audited, but simple marker-weight, 5Mb block and genomewide burden routes do not add robust structure beyond relatedness-like signals and matched controls.

Boundary. These results do not show that PlantCAD2 or AgroNT lack biological signal. They show that the tested G2F 2,383-marker functional-prior routes did not pass frozen predictive diagnostics.

Future Directions

  • Move from sparse marker-weight kernels to prespecified gene-set burden tests using maize gene annotations and stress-response gene sets.
  • Add LD/block-aware sensitivity analysis so functional scores are aggregated over haplotype blocks rather than arbitrary single markers.
  • Test denser imputed variants or WGS-derived markers before concluding that sequence foundation models cannot help breeding prediction.
  • Keep GPN / PhyloGPN / GPN-Star as a feasibility-first evolutionary-constraint branch until maize/B73-centered alignment and checkpoint assumptions are verified.
