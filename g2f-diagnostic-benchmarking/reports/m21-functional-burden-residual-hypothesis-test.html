Interpretation

[Inference] The result does not mean PlantCAD2 or AgroNT lack biological signal. It means the tested 2024 sparse-marker score-to-burden route did not create reliable incremental predictive structure after a strong frozen G2F baseline.

[Conclusion] The portfolio contribution is the controlled bridge from DNA foundation-model variant scores to maize GxE prediction features, plus a clear negative result under frozen controls.
